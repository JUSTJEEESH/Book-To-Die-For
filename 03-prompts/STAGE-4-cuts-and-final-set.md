## Method

Each part was cut in four passes:

1. **Overlap pass.** Where two prompts produce the same answer, keep the one with the sharper wording or the better position in the arc. The thirteen clusters flagged in Stage 3 were resolved first.
2. **Nine-test pass.** Anything passing only one test, or passing tests weakly, was cut unless it did a structural job (an easy opener, a laugh in a heavy section).
3. **Read-aloud pass.** Each surviving prompt was read as if across a table. Anything that sounded like a form, a therapist, or a greeting card was cut or rewritten.
4. **Form-variety pass.** Prompts were ordered so no two consecutive prompts in a part share a form. Where the order forced a clash, the prompt was rewritten into a different form rather than moved out of its right place in the arc. Rewrites are listed below.

Two budget adjustments were made against Stage 2: Part Eight gives up one full page (12 to 11)
and Part Eleven gains it (8 to 9), because Part Eleven is the centerpiece and had more prompts
worth keeping than it had pages. Total page count is unchanged.

## Audit checks passed

- No prompt begins "What was your favorite."
- No two consecutive prompts in any part share a form.
- No prompt assumes a spouse, children, grandchildren, faith, culture, or politics. The two belief prompts (8-05, 8-20) are asked without assumption and marked optional on the page.
- Every part opens on its easiest prompt and places its spread between 60% and 80% of the way through, except Part Eleven, which has two spreads: one at the middle and one as the closer, by design.
- Marked prompts: 19 of 202, roughly one in ten. Lighter than the one-in-five planned, because when a marked prompt is rare it carries more weight. Every part has at least one.
- Humor is present in every part of Movement I and II, and in Parts Nine and Twelve. Part Six and Part Seven have none by design; their quick pages provide the release instead.
- Read-aloud pass completed on all 202. Eleven prompts were tightened during the pass; those edits are in the manuscript.
