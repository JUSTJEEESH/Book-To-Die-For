# STAGE 3 — Master Prompt Inventory

**Status:** Awaiting approval (Stage 4 cuts follow)
**Candidates:** 282 written prompts, plus 13 family-question pages and 8 letters
**Target after Stage 4:** 208

Every prompt is written as it would appear on the page. A second line in italics is the sub-line
printed beneath the prompt in smaller type. Nothing here is a note to be written later.

## Legend

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ID | Part and number |
| Page | **F** full page · **S** two-page spread · **Q** quick one (one or two lines) |
| ★ | A marked prompt: "the questions nobody thinks to ask" |
| Form | TMA Tell me about · DYR Do you remember · WTE Was there ever a time · FTS Finish this sentence · WHO Who was/is · DESC Describe/Walk me through · Q direct question · STMT statement heading (Parts 7 and 11) |
| Tests | Which of the nine tests it passes: 1 reveals the unknown · 2 vivid memory · 3 personality · 4 emotion · 5 story · 6 wisdom · 7 preserves what would vanish · 8 future connection · 9 worth keeping |

Rules applied while writing:
- No two consecutive prompts in a part share a form.
- No prompt begins "What was your favorite."
- No prompt assumes a spouse, children, grandchildren, faith, or a particular culture.
- Each part opens with its easiest prompt and places its spread about two-thirds through.
- Skip-permission is built into wording where the subject is hard, and Part Six carries it on every page.

- 1-04/1-05 (parents as people) vs 8-14 (what parents believed): keep both, different jobs.
- 2-19 (road almost taken at twenty) vs 4-07 (risk not taken) vs 10-01 (chance not taken): three doors into one room. Keep 4-07 as the marked prompt; rewrite 2-19 to be strictly about a place or person; cut 10-01 or narrow it.

- 5-09 (noticed, never told) vs 11-15 (hope you know I noticed): keep 11-15, cut 5-09, or make 5-09 about a specific childhood moment.
