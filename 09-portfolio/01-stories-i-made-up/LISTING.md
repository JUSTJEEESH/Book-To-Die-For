## Description (HTML for KDP)

<b>Somewhere in your house there's a dragon nobody else has ever heard of.</b>

You made it up at nine o'clock at night, with one hand on a small back. You've told the story fifty times. You couldn't repeat it now if you tried.

<b>The Stories I Made Up</b> is a keepsake for the bedtime stories a parent invents. Not the stories in other books. Yours. The recurring cast. The one that only works if you do the voice. The ones that were mostly a list of animals.

<b>Inside:</b>
<ul>
<li>Eight cast pages: name, what they are, what they always say, the trouble they get into, who they're secretly based on, and a drawing box for the listener</li>
<li>Thirty-six story spreads, each with a title line, the date you first told it, a row of boxes to tick every time it's asked for, ruled pages, and a drawing box</li>
<li>Where the stories happened, and how they always began and ended</li>
<li>The one they always asked for, written the long way</li>
<li>Stories that never got finished, and the listener's own stories</li>
<li>The last one: a story written now, for a night you might not be there</li>
<li>A pre-numbered list at the back, so anyone can find a story again</li>
</ul>

<b>For dads, moms, grandparents, and anyone who has ever been asked for "the truck one" again.</b> Fill it in over months, one story a night. Let the kids draw. Write in pen, pencil, or crayon.

Nobody else has ever heard these. That's what makes them theirs.

<b>Write them down while you still remember how they go.</b>
