# THE STORIES I MADE UP — Brief (Stages 1 and 2)

**Title:** The Stories I Made Up
**Tagline:** Every story I ever told you, written down.
**Series:** Words to Keep · **Author:** Joshua Caleb Green
**Format:** 7 x 10 paperback, cream, matte, 116 pages, no bleed. Hardcover same interior.
**Price:** $18.99 paperback (print about $2.97, royalty about $8.42), $26.99 hardcover.
**KDP:** publish without ISBN (low-content classification expected). Carousel and A+ built from a proof.
**Launch:** live by October 20, 2026 for Christmas; then Father's Day and new-dad gifting year-round.

## Positioning

A keepsake for the bedtime stories a parent invents. Not a story book: the book of *your* stories,
the ones with the dragon who was afraid of stairs, told fifty times and never written anywhere.
The parent writes them down, one to a spread, over months. The child draws the cast. Years later
it is the object the grown child keeps.

## Buyer and writer

- **Buyer:** a mother buying for the father of a 3-to-8-year-old (Father's Day, Christmas, a new-baby gift), or a grandparent who tells stories on sleepovers, or a parent buying for themselves after realizing they can't remember last year's stories.
- **Writer:** the storyteller. Usually a dad. The listing speaks to the buyer; the book speaks to the writer.
- **Completion:** no completion problem. Each story is one sitting, and the child asks for it. The "told" tally makes the book part of bedtime itself.

## Differentiation

Nothing on Amazon does this. The adjacent products are write-and-draw books for kids and
personalized printed story books. Ours is the parent's invented canon, with the cast pages, the
tally boxes, the drawing boxes, a place for abandoned stories, a place for the child's own stories,
and a final story written for a night the storyteller might not be there.

## Structure (116 pages)

**Front matter (10):** half title · blank · title · copyright · *If you're giving this book* (recto) ·
*The storyteller and the listener* (verso, facing the opening letter) · *These are yours* (opening
letter) · *How to use this book* · *The rules* · blank

**Part One — The Cast (12):** opener · 8 cast pages (name, what they are, what they always say, the
trouble they get into, who they're secretly based on, a drawing box) · *Where the stories happened*
(drawing box and lines) · *How we started, how we ended* (four fields) · blank

**Part Two — The Stories (74):** opener · 36 story spreads. Left page: title line, first told, told to,
a row of twelve tally boxes, ruled lines. Right page: ruled lines and a drawing box with "drawn by."

**Part Three — The Ones We Kept (18):** opener · *The one you always asked for* (4 pp) · *Stories that
never got finished* (2 pp) · *Stories you made up yourself* (4 pp, for the listener) · *Things you said
that went into the stories* (quick page) · *What I remember about these* (2 pp, for the listener, years
later) · *The last one* (2 pp: a story written now for a night the storyteller might not be there)

**Closing (2):** *For the one reading this* · *The list* (a pre-numbered table of contents the writer fills in with the story titles) · blank to even

## Voice

Warm, dry, unhurried. The book knows what nine o'clock at night with one hand on a small back is
like. No exclamation marks. No "magical." No "precious." The word "adventure" appears nowhere.
