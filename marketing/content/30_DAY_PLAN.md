# The First 30 Days: 2026-09-22 to 2026-10-21

Goal for the month: an account that posts every day, a listing that converts, the first twenty
reviews, and enough data to know which three concepts deserve ad money in November.

Not a goal for the month: sales volume. With zero reviews and no A+, sales are a by-product this
month. Christmas is the first real window, and this month builds the machine that will run it.

## Week 0 (Sept 22 to 24): set up

- Register @wordstokeep (or the fallback) on TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Pinterest, Facebook, Threads.
  Same profile image, same bio family (`../accounts/ACCOUNT_STRATEGY.md`).
- Build the link page: Amazon Attribution link to the book; the ten questions as a PDF (one page,
  the book's list, the brand's type); an email sign-up.
- Create the Amazon Attribution tags: one per platform for the bio link, one per ad later.
- Order six author copies of the paperback and two of the hardcover (print cost). These are the
  props for every photograph and video.
- Photograph the book for the Amazon carousel (`../amazon/AMAZON_CONVERSION.md`, section 2) and
  submit the A+ modules. Update the description opening.
- Make the sign-off frame (1.5 s) and the S10 nightly template.
- Start the Amazon automatic Sponsored Products campaign at $10 a day.

## What "working" looks like at day 30

| Signal | Target | Why this number |
|---|---|---|
| Posts | 30 on TikTok, 20 Reels, 12 carousels, 30 Facebook, 20 pins | A daily habit, not a burst |
| Followers | Not a target. Shares are. | Followers follow shares |
| Shares per post (median, TikTok) | Rising week over week | The brand's behavior |
| Comments containing a story | 100 total | The research feed |
| Reviews on Amazon | 20 | The conversion floor for ads |
| Amazon images and A+ | Live | Conversion |
| Creator posts live | 3 to 5 | Real footage for November ads |
| Attribution clicks | 500 | Enough to read a click-to-page rate |
| Ad learning | One hook with a thumb-stop rate above 30% | The Christmas creative |

## What to stop at day 30

- Any series with three below-median posts in a row.
- Any platform that has produced no shares and no attribution clicks (likely Threads or X).
- Any ad concept that costs more than $2 per attribution click at stage 1.
