# Production package · C-02 · Something with a brand name

**TITLE.** Not a sunset. Something with a brand name.
**OBJECTIVE.** The account's first comment engine and the best candidate for a Spark Ad. Introduce the When You Miss Me register: practical, funny, then tender.
**TARGET AUDIENCE.** Everyone; strongest with Segment C (the ones who waited too long) and Segment A.

**HOOK.** "Not a sunset. Something with a brand name."

**0-3 SECOND SHOT.** Top-down, a kitchen counter, daylight. A hand sets down a tin of a specific brand of mints (real object, label visible). Text, cream on a soft ink band across the top: "Not a sunset."

**FULL SCRIPT (16 s).**
- 0.0 to 1.5: the mints. Text: "Not a sunset."
- 1.5 to 3.0: a can of a specific coffee brand set down beside it. Text: "Something with a brand name."
- 3.0 to 4.2: a bar of a specific soap.
- 4.2 to 5.4: a car air freshener, the tree kind.
- 5.4 to 6.6: a jar of a specific hand cream.
- 6.6 to 7.8: a pack of a specific chewing gum.
- 7.8 to 9.0: a bottle of a specific aftershave.
- 9.0 to 10.2: a box of a specific tea.
- 10.2 to 13.5: wide, all eight objects in a row. Text, larger, centered: "The dumb, specific thing that should remind you of me."
- 13.5 to 14.5: text under it, small: "Part Twelve."
- 14.5 to 16.0: sign-off frame.

**VOICEOVER.** None. Room tone and the sound of each object touching the counter.

**ON-SCREEN TEXT.** "Not a sunset." / "Something with a brand name." / "The dumb, specific thing that should remind you of me." / "Part Twelve." / sign-off.

**SHOT LIST.**
1. Top-down locked-off, 50 mm equivalent, counter fills the frame, hand enters from the bottom each time (1.2 s each, 8 shots).
2. Wide top-down of all eight objects (3.3 s).
3. Sign-off frame (1.5 s).

**AI VIDEO PROMPTS.** Not applicable. Real objects are the point; the labels must be real brands (fair to show; we make no claims about them).

**EDITING NOTES.** Hard cuts on the object touching the counter. No transitions. Keep the hand consistent (same person, same sleeve). Color: warm daylight, slightly desaturated. Export 1080 x 1920.

**SOUND/MUSIC DIRECTION.** Original audio only: the clink and tap of each object. No music. This also keeps the post boostable.

**CAPTION.**
TikTok: "What's the brand-name thing that reminds you of your dad? Your mom? We're collecting them. #askthemtonight #griefjourney #familyhistory #whenyoumissme"
Instagram: "Not a sunset. Something with a brand name. What's yours?"

**CTA.** The question in the caption.

**PINNED COMMENT.** "Tell us the brand-name thing. First names only if you want to be in the roundup."

**THUMBNAIL/TEXT FRAME.** The eight objects in a row with "Something with a brand name."

**ALTERNATIVE VERSION (A, emotional).** Same objects, slower (2 s each), one line of quiet piano (Commercial Music Library), and the text at the end changes to the book's full sub-line: "Not a sunset. Something with a brand name. Or a smell."

**WHY IT SHOULD BE TESTED.** Grief content online is almost all sad; this is the funny, useful corner nobody else occupies. The comments it produces are the source for C-46 and for months of roundups, and the object montage is the cheapest ad creative we can make.
