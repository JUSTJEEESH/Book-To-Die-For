# Production package · C-07 · Four pages, no prompt

**TITLE.** Four pages. No prompt.
**OBJECTIVE.** The product's strongest image, as a video. Retargeting creative (AD-04) and the post that turns followers into buyers.
**TARGET AUDIENCE.** Segment A. Retargeting: anyone who watched 50% of another post.

**HOOK.** No words. A blank ruled page and a pen that does not move.

**0-3 SECOND SHOT.** Top-down on the printed book, open to "One last letter" (Part Thirteen). Cream page, gray rules, the heading in the book's type. A fine ballpoint held above the page, still. Daylight from the left. Nothing happens.

**FULL SCRIPT (20 s).**
- 0.0 to 6.0: the pen hovers. Very slight tremor. No text.
- 6.0 to 9.0: the pen lowers, writes "To" and the first letter of a name, and stops.
- 9.0 to 12.0: the pen lifts. Hold on the page: "To M".
- 12.0 to 15.0: text, cream on a soft ink band: "Four pages. No prompt."
- 15.0 to 18.0: text: "Most people write this one last."
- 18.0 to 20.0: sign-off frame.

**VOICEOVER.** None. Version A adds one line at 12 s, read quietly: "It's the last thing in the book. Everything before it gets them there."

**ON-SCREEN TEXT.** "Four pages. No prompt." / "Most people write this one last." / sign-off.

**SHOT LIST.** One unbroken shot, locked off, 20 s, 50 mm equivalent, f/2.8 feel, then the sign-off. The hand belongs to a real older person if one is available (a parent, a grandparent, with permission), otherwise the team's own. No ring, no watch, no nail polish that dates it.

**AI VIDEO PROMPTS.** Not applicable. The page must be the real printed book. (The AI version of this idea is AV-02 "The Empty Chair" in `../ai-video/AI_VIDEO_IDEAS.md`, which is conceptual and says so.)

**EDITING NOTES.** No cuts before the sign-off. The tremor is the performance; do not stabilize it away. Grade warm, cream reading as cream (not white). Export 1080 x 1920.

**SOUND/MUSIC DIRECTION.** Room tone; the scratch of the pen on "To" at 6 s should be audible. No music. If boosted, keep it silent.

**CAPTION.**
TikTok: "The last section of the book has no questions. Just names and room. Who would yours be to? #askthemtonight #familyhistory #lettersfrommom #griefjourney"
Instagram: "Four pages. No prompt. Most people write this one last."

**CTA.** Pinned only.

**PINNED COMMENT.** "The last letter is page 245. There's a page for everyone before it. Link in bio."

**THUMBNAIL/TEXT FRAME.** The page with "To M" written, pen lifted, no text overlay.

**ALTERNATIVE VERSION (H, question only).** Same shot, no text at all until the sign-off. 15 s. Tests whether the image alone carries it.

**WHY IT SHOULD BE TESTED.** Every other post shows a question. This one shows the answer's absence, and the book as an object. If it wins on saves and attribution clicks, it is the retargeting ad for every season; if it loses, the product post belongs later in the funnel than we thought.
