# Future Products: the brand beyond one paperback

The rule from the decision log stands: nothing launches until the flagship has 100 reviews. The
audience comes first. This is the order in which the audience, once it exists, would buy.

| Order | Product | Why the audience would buy it | Built already? | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hardcover of the flagship | Already live at $28.99; the Christmas format | Yes | Make it the December default on the listing |
| 2 | Conversation cards ("The questions nobody thinks to ask") | The account's nightly question, as a deck for the dinner table; low price, high gifting, fits a stocking | No | Not printable on KDP; a print-on-demand card vendor or a short run; the first non-book product |
| 3 | Role editions (Mom, Dad, Grandma, Grandpa) | Search-shaped titles; the interior is identical | Pipeline exists (`06-cover/build_cover.py --title`) | Naming that keeps the parent's voice: "What I Want You to Know. Love, Mom." Decide after 100 reviews per the log |
| 4 | Lo Que Quiero Que Sepas (Spanish) | Empty premium slot; Día de las Madres | Yes (`09-portfolio/03`); needs a native editor's pass | May 2027 |
| 5 | The Stories I Made Up | New-dad and Christmas gift; the most shareable object | Yes (`09-portfolio/01`); needs cover art | The only portfolio book that could be live for Christmas 2026 |
| 6 | Still Me | Memory-care families; evergreen; professionals buy in bulk | Yes (`09-portfolio/02`); needs cover art | January 2027 |
| 7 | The Words We Brought With Us | Immigrant families; the sayings and songs | Yes (`09-portfolio/04`) | April 2027 |
| 8 | Read This When (letters for the days I might not be there) | Graduation and wedding gifting; a weekend to write | Brief only (`08-extensions`) | Spring 2027 |
| 9 | What We Want You to Know (couples) | Anniversaries; nothing like it in print | Brief only | 2027 |
| 10 | Printable / digital version | For families outside Amazon's print reach; email-list product | No | Low priority; the handwriting is the product |
| 11 | Guided audio version | The "voice" counter-position, done our way: the book's questions read aloud, one a day, for the writer to answer on paper | No | A podcast feed is the cheapest form |
| 12 | Family interview cards for kids ("Ask your grandparent this") | The S8 series as a product | No | 2027 |
| 13 | Gift bundles (flagship plus cards; Mom plus Grandma) | Two-copy behavior seen in the category | Amazon "virtual bundles" need Brand Registry | After a trademark |
| 14 | Subscription or community | Only if the email list asks for it | No | Not before 10,000 subscribers |

## What we will not build

Anything that dilutes the flagship before it has its reviews; anything with envelopes, pockets, or
seals (KDP cannot); a daily or weekly schedule format; a personalized-print product that needs a
fulfillment operation before the brand can fund one.
