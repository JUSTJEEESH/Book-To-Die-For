# Static and Carousel Concepts

Twenty concepts for Instagram, Pinterest, Facebook, and Threads. Every one uses the book's own type
system (Cormorant Garamond for headings, EB Garamond for body, cream #efe7d6 on ink #1e2838, or
charcoal on bone #f3efe6) so the feed looks like the book. Photographs are of the real printed book,
real hands, real paper. No stock families. No AI people.

Template rule: a static post is one idea. A carousel is one idea per slide with the last slide the
sign-off frame ("The questions nobody thinks to ask. Words to Keep.") and never a "buy now."

### ST-01 · Ten pages (carousel, 11 slides)
Slide 1: "If you only fill out ten pages." Slides 2 to 11: one prompt each, exactly as printed:
Tell me about your mother, not as a mother. · The story your family tells at every gathering. Now the
version that actually happened. · The first time you fell in love. · Tell me about a kind of love you
didn't expect. · What got you through? · Something I never said because I thought you already knew.
· The rule you live by that you've never said out loud. · What I want you to know about me that has
nothing to do with being your parent. · A tradition to start after I'm gone. · One last letter.
Last line on slide 11: "Nobody finishes a book like this. Ten pages is a treasure."
Caption: "Start with these. Send it to the one who'll actually ask." Pinned comment: the link.

### ST-02 · Not as a mother (static)
Ink ground. Cream serif, large: "Tell me about your mother, not as a mother." Small, below: "What
was she like as a person? What did she want?" Corner: the sign-off. Caption: one line, "Ask her
tonight." Threads version is text only.

### ST-03 · The usual question / ours (carousel, 6 slides)
Each slide split top/bottom. Top, gray: the standard question. Bottom, cream: the book's. Pairs:
"What was your favorite food?" / "What did the kitchen smell like on an ordinary weeknight?" ·
"What did you do for work?" / "What did you actually do all day? Not the title. The Tuesday." ·
"What's the secret to a long marriage?" / "What is a long relationship made of? Not the advice. The
specifics." · "What advice would you give your younger self?" / "Write a note to yourself at
thirty." · "What are you most proud of?" / "Tell me about a day you didn't quit." Slide 6: "Most
memory journals ask the same fifty questions. This one asks the two hundred that come after." Never
name a competitor.

### ST-04 · How to give a book like this (carousel, 7 slides)
From the giver's guide, one instruction per slide, quoted: Fill in the next page before you wrap
it. Say why. · Write one question of your own at the end of every part. · When you give it, ask them
one question out loud. The first answer is the hardest, and you can take it for them. · Show them the
page called If you only fill out ten pages. · Don't ask when it'll be done. Ask what they've written
lately. Slide 6: a photo of the giver's page filled in. Slide 7: sign-off.

### ST-05 · Twelve questions for the drive home (static list)
Tall Pinterest format. Twelve quick ones from Parts One to Three that work in a car. Header: "For
the drive home." Footer: sign-off. Seasonal: Thanksgiving week and December 20 to 24.

### ST-06 · The brand-name thing (carousel of objects, 8 slides)
Slide 1: "Not a sunset. Something with a brand name." Slides 2 to 7: photographs of ordinary
branded objects on a kitchen counter, no caption on the image. Slide 8: "The dumb, specific thing
that should remind you of me." Caption asks for theirs.

### ST-07 · The giver's page, four ways (4-up grid)
Four real photographs of the giver's page filled in: "To Mom, by Sarah" / "To Dad, by all of us" /
"To Grandma, by the cousins" / "To my husband, by me." Real handwriting, real first names with
permission (or the team's own, honestly). Headline: "One book. Every one of them." Reused as A+
module 3 on Amazon.

### ST-08 · Things I never said, finish these (carousel, 7 slides)
Slide 1: "Finish these." Slides 2 to 7, one stem each with a blank: I never told you that ______ ·
I was wrong about ______ · I always meant to ______ · I'm sorry I ______ · Thank you for ______. I
don't think I ever said it. · I hope you know that ______. Caption: "Finish one in the comments, or
finish it to them."

### ST-09 · Questions for your dad that aren't about you (static list)
Five prompts: The worst boss you ever had. · What did you do for money that never went on a résumé?
· And the risk you didn't take. · What's the dumbest thing you did that you survived? · Who was the
adult who saw something in you? Footer: "Ask one. Clear your afternoon."

### ST-10 · The year you were born (carousel, 7 slides)
The six era prompts, one per slide: What a loaf of bread cost, or a gallon of gas, or a movie
ticket. · The song that was on every radio. · What the grown-ups were worried about. · Who I was
named after, or where the name came from. · The first news event I remember hearing about. ·
Something everyone had then that nobody has now. Slide 7: "Ask them. Then look it up and see who's right."

### ST-11 · Which of these ten would she refuse? (static)
The ten-page list as a numbered column with small empty circles. Header: "Which one would your mom
skip?" Comments do the rest.

### ST-12 · The sentence at the door (static)
Bone ground, charcoal type: "What I always said at the door." Below, small: "Your family's version
of 'I love you' is probably a sentence at the door. What's yours?"

### ST-13 · Being right, or being kind (static quote)
The prompt with its sub-line. Facebook-native. No CTA.

### ST-14 · Ask your grandparent this, Sunday edition (weekly template)
Fixed frame: "Ask her this on Sunday." One prompt. Date in small type. The same layout every week
so it is recognizable in a feed.

### ST-15 · What's inside (carousel, 8 slides)
The honest spec, photographed: 7 x 10, the size next to a hand · one prompt per page, 21-point type ·
a two-page spread · a quick-ones page · the marked-prompt ring, explained · a family-question page ·
the letters section · the index. Caption: "Made to be written in, and kept." This is the product
carousel; post at most twice a month. Reused for Amazon images.

### ST-16 · The one to skip (static)
"The song to play. And the one to skip, because it will wreck you." Caption: "Name the one to skip."
Follow-up post: the public playlist built from the comments.

### ST-17 · Skip this one if you want (static)
The Part Six skip line, reproduced as printed: "Skip this one if you want. Nothing is lost."
Caption: "It's printed on every page of the hardest section."

### ST-18 · The story behind this photograph (carousel template)
Slide 1: the photograph (creator-supplied). Slides 2 to 5: one question each over a crop of the
photo. Slide 6: the answer, in the family's words, first names only. Template shared with creators.

### ST-19 · Retirement: not the title, the Tuesday (static)
LinkedIn-safe layout, bone ground. "What did you actually do all day at work? Not the title. The
Tuesday." Small: "For the one who's retiring." No emoji. No hashtags beyond two.

### ST-20 · The last letter (static, product)
Photograph, from above: the "One last letter" page, empty, the book's ruled cream page, a pen laid
diagonally. Text: "Four pages. No prompt." This is the strongest single still the brand owns. Use
it as the Pinterest pin that links to Amazon, the Meta retargeting image, and Amazon image slot 6.
