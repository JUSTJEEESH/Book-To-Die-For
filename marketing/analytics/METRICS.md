# Metrics: what we measure and why

Ranked by how much each one tells us. Read them in this order every week.

| Rank | Metric | Source | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Purchases per Amazon Attribution tag | Amazon Ads console, Attribution | The only number that ties content to a sale |
| 2 | Reviews (count, average) | Amazon page | The conversion floor; ads do not work under 20 |
| 3 | Shares and sends per post | TikTok, Instagram insights | The behavior the brand is built for |
| 4 | Story comments (a comment with a specific detail) | Manual count, COMMENT_LOG.md | The research feed and the proof of resonance |
| 5 | Saves | Platform insights | Intent to use with a parent |
| 6 | Amazon sessions and conversion rate | KDP reports, Amazon Ads | Whether the page converts the traffic |
| 7 | Attribution clicks per tag | Amazon Attribution | Which post or ad drives traffic |
| 8 | Watch-through (average % watched) | Platform insights | Hook and pacing quality |
| 9 | ACoS on Amazon Ads | KDP Ads | Profitability of on-Amazon spend (target 35% launch, 25% after) |
| 10 | Cost per attributed purchase (off-Amazon ads) | Meta/TikTok spend ÷ Attribution purchases | Break-even is about $6 per paperback sale |
| 11 | Views | Platform insights | Reach; least informative on its own |
| 12 | Followers | Platform insights | A lagging indicator of shares |

## Files

Drop weekly exports into this folder as `YYYY-MM-DD-<platform>.csv`. The weekly review reads them.

## Break-even math (2026-09-21)

| Format | List | Royalty |
|---|---|---|
| Paperback | $17.99 (launch) | $5.47 |
| Paperback | $19.99 | $6.67 |
| Hardcover | $28.99 | about $7.50 |

An ad costing more than the royalty per attributed purchase loses money on the first sale. It may
still be worth running for reviews and rank during a launch window; say so explicitly in the
experiment when that is the reason.
