# Weekly Marketing Review

Run every Monday with `/weekly-marketing`. One entry per week, newest at the top. The engine learns
only if this file is honest: real numbers, real losers, and a decision at the end of every entry.

## Template

```
## Week of YYYY-MM-DD

### Numbers
| Platform | Posts | Views | Shares | Saves | Story comments | Attribution clicks | Purchases |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Amazon: reviews ___ (avg ___), BSR ___, sessions ___, conversion ___ %, ad spend $___, ACoS ___ %.

### Top-performing content (by shares, then story comments)
1. ID, title, platform, the number, and the one reason it worked.
2.
3.

### Worst-performing content
1. ID, title, platform, the number, and the honest reason.
2.
3.

### Patterns
- Hooks: which structure won this week (curiosity / humor / confession / …).
- Topics: which pillar produced the stories.
- CTAs: which line produced sends.
- Formats: text vs. object vs. real footage vs. carousel.

### Audience insights
- What people said in the comments that we did not expect.
- Who is showing up (segment), from the replies and the follower demographics.

### Decisions
- Repeat: (IDs) with the version to repeat.
- Kill: (IDs) and why.
- Improve: (IDs) and the one change.
- New series opportunity: (from a comment thread or a repeated question).
- New experiments: (added to experiments/EXPERIMENTS.md).
- Ads: which organic winner moves to stage 1; which ad moves stages; which stops.
- Creators: who posted; who to follow up; who to drop.
```
