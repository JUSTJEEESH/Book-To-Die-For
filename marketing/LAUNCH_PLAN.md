# LAUNCH PLAN: What I Want You to Know

The one document to open. What we are doing, why, in what order, and what it will cost. Dated
2026-09-21. The book has been live for one day.

## Where we are

The book is good. The listing is a cover and a paragraph. Nobody knows it exists. There are zero
reviews, no photographs of the inside, no account posting the questions, no ads, no creators. The
first gift season is thirteen weeks away.

The target is a major Amazon bestseller and a brand that can sell millions of dollars of books and
related products over time. That is the direction we are building toward, not a promise. What is
true today: the category is proven (the leader has sold hundreds of thousands of copies at a lower
price with worse questions), the product is better on every dimension a buyer can see except
binding, and the marketing position is empty (nobody owns the questions themselves, or handwriting).

## The strategy in five lines

1. Build a faceless account, Words to Keep, that posts the questions nobody thinks to ask, one a night, and what people's parents said back.
2. Make the Amazon page convert: photographs of the inside, A+ modules, the first twenty reviews.
3. Turn organic winners into ads, staged, with Amazon Attribution on every link, starting only when reviews exist.
4. Put the book in the hands of real families through creators, gifting first, and let their honest footage become the ads.
5. Learn weekly. Repeat what earns sends. Kill what does not. Never repeat an idea with different words.

## Phase 3 · Mother's Day 2027 (Mar 1 to May 10)

The largest single window in the category. Orders peak 10 to 3 days before the day.

- March: the account should have a body of real "my mom said" content and real creator footage. Build the Mother's Day creative from the year's best (C-42, AD-09, C-29 footage).
- April 5: ads live. April 20: Amazon bids doubled. Image 3 swapped to a "To Mom" giver's page.
- If the flagship has 100+ reviews by March, the Mom edition decision is taken then, not before (`future-products/ROADMAP.md`).

Father's Day follows the same shape, four weeks later, led by the funny prompts (C-41, AD-03).

## Phase 4 · The brand (2027 onward)

Once the audience exists and the flagship has its reviews: the conversation cards (the account's
nightly question as a product), the role editions with names that keep the parent's voice, the
Spanish edition, the portfolio books already built, and the letters book. The order and the reasons
are in `future-products/ROADMAP.md`. Nothing launches before the flagship earns it.

The category leader took eight years and twenty-one editions. A KDP-grade Mom journal at $24.99 is
reported to move about 7,000 copies a month at the top of the category. That ceiling is real. The
path to it runs through reviews, sends, and seasons, in that order.

## What we will not do

- Post anything invented. Not a comment, not a reaction, not a family.
- Use a clock. "Before it's too late" is banned everywhere.
- Scale ads before reviews exist.
- Launch a second edition before the first has 100 reviews.
- Make the author the face.
- Sell into grief. The people who waited too long are the reason the brand exists; they are never a target.
