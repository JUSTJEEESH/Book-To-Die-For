# Paid Ad Creative Engine

Ten launch ad concepts for Meta (Facebook and Instagram), TikTok, and Pinterest, plus the Amazon
Ads structure. Every organic winner becomes an ad; nothing runs as an ad before an organic version
has been posted at least once, except the three "conversion" ads marked as such.

## Principles

- **Where the click goes.** Amazon, through an Amazon Attribution link (free in the Amazon Ads
  console for KDP authors; one tag per ad per platform). Without attribution we cannot tell which
  ad sold a book. The Amazon page converts better than any landing page we could build this year
  because it carries reviews and Prime shipping.
- **Diversified creative.** TikTok's own guidance is to keep several creative variations live per
  ad group rather than one asset; we run at least 3 to 5 variations per concept and let the platform
  find the winner. One concept, many versions (see `../content/TESTING_MATRIX.md`).
- **No clock, no fake scarcity, no invented reactions.** The same voice rules as organic. Ad review
  teams also reject "before they die" framing in this category, so the rule costs nothing.
- **Zero-review reality.** Until the listing has 20 or more reviews, ads convert poorly. September
  and October budgets are for learning (which hook, which audience), not for scale. Scale starts
  November 1 for Christmas and April 1 for Mother's Day, if reviews are in place.
- **Budget reality.** Royalty is $5.47 at $17.99 and $6.67 at $19.99 on the paperback, about $7.50
  on the hardcover. An ad that costs more than about $6 per Amazon sale loses money on the first
  sale. Break-even is judged per purchase, not per click.

## The ten concepts

### AD-01 · Not as a mother (conversion, text video)
- **Hook.** "Tell me about your mother, not as a mother."
- **Body.** Three sub-questions appear one at a time: "What was she like as a person?" "What did she want?" "What did she get away with?" Then the book, closed, on a table. Then: "202 questions she'll actually answer. In her own hand."
- **CTA.** "See the book" (Amazon).
- **Video concept.** C-01. Text on ink, 12 s, optional quiet piano.
- **Target audience.** Meta: women 35-58, interests: genealogy, family history, gifts for mom, Ancestry, journaling; plus a lookalike of Amazon Attribution converters once there are 100. TikTok: women 30-55, interest "family," "gift ideas."
- **Landing.** Amazon paperback (hardcover in December).
- **Test variable.** Sound (none vs. piano) and the last line ("In her own hand" vs. "The last section is a letter. To you.").

### AD-02 · Something with a brand name (awareness to conversion)
- **Hook.** "Not a sunset. Something with a brand name."
- **Body.** Object montage (C-02). Then: "The dumb, specific thing that should remind you of me." Then: "There's a page for it."
- **CTA.** "The book."
- **Target.** Broad, 28-60, both sexes. TikTok Spark Ad on the organic post once it has comments.
- **Landing.** Amazon.
- **Test variable.** Objects (coffee vs. mints vs. soap first); with and without the page shown.

### AD-03 · He'll say he doesn't have any stories (Father's Day, and evergreen for men)
- **Hook.** "Your dad is going to say he doesn't have any stories."
- **Body.** If C-03 footage exists and is permissioned: the father talking. If not: text over a chair at a kitchen table: "Ask him about the worst boss he ever had. Clear your afternoon." Then the book.
- **CTA.** "Give him this anyway."
- **Target.** Women 30-55 (they buy for fathers); men 30-50 with interests in dad jokes, tools, sports nostalgia. Peak: May 15 to June 12.
- **Landing.** Amazon.
- **Test variable.** Funny prompt shown first (worst boss vs. dumbest thing survived vs. money not on a résumé).

### AD-04 · Four pages, no prompt (retargeting)
- **Hook.** [The empty ruled page and the hovering pen.]
- **Body.** "Four pages. No prompt. Most people write this one last." Then: "It's the last thing in the book. Everything before it gets them there."
- **CTA.** "See inside."
- **Target.** Retargeting only: viewers of any 50%+ video view, Instagram engagers, and Amazon Attribution clickers who did not buy (Meta pixel cannot see Amazon, so use engagement audiences).
- **Landing.** Amazon.
- **Test variable.** 15 s vs. 25 s hold before the text.

### AD-05 · The usual question vs. ours (comparison, conversion)
- **Hook.** "'What advice would you give your younger self' is the worst question you can ask a parent."
- **Body.** Three side-by-side pairs (ST-03). Last: "Most memory journals ask the same fifty questions. This one asks the two hundred that come after."
- **CTA.** "The two hundred."
- **Target.** Meta: people who engaged with journaling, memoir, family-history content; Amazon shoppers interest. TikTok: "books," "gift ideas."
- **Landing.** Amazon.
- **Test variable.** Which pair leads (kitchen smell vs. the Tuesday).

### AD-06 · Don't ask her what she wants for Christmas (seasonal)
- **Hook.** "Don't ask your mom what she wants for Christmas."
- **Body.** "Ask her this instead." Three prompts. "Then give her the book they came from." Hardcover shown.
- **CTA.** "The hardcover."
- **Target.** Women 30-58, gift intent, November 10 to December 12. Pinterest promoted pin from November 1.
- **Landing.** Amazon hardcover.
- **Test variable.** Hardcover vs. paperback shown; "Christmas" in hook vs. not.

### AD-07 · How to give a book like this (carousel, objection ad)
- **Hook.** "How to give a book like this so it actually gets written in."
- **Body.** Five cards from the giver's guide (ST-04). Last card: "It's built in. Page 5."
- **CTA.** "See the book."
- **Target.** Meta carousel; women 35-58; retarget engagers.
- **Landing.** Amazon.
- **Test variable.** Card order (start with "fill in the first page" vs. "don't ask when it'll be done").

### AD-08 · Ten pages (save-and-buy)
- **Hook.** "Nobody finishes a book like this. Ten pages is a treasure."
- **Body.** The ten, fast. Then the book. "It says that on page nine."
- **CTA.** "Start with ten."
- **Target.** Broad; this ad recruits the skeptical buyer ("my dad won't do it").
- **Landing.** Amazon.
- **Test variable.** Honest line first vs. list first.

### AD-09 · You've asked her everything except the things that matter (Mother's Day)
- **Hook.** "You've asked her everything except the things that matter."
- **Body.** "Something I never said because I thought you already knew." "Who do you still talk to in your head?" "The first time you fell in love. Not the story you tell." Then: "Give her the questions. Keep her answers."
- **CTA.** "For Mother's Day."
- **Target.** Women 28-55, April 5 to May 8. Orders peak 10 to 3 days before the day; budget weighted to those days.
- **Landing.** Amazon.
- **Test variable.** Three prompts vs. one; "Keep her answers" vs. "Their words, for you."

### AD-10 · So the baby will have it (new grandparents)
- **Hook.** "So the baby will have it."
- **Body.** Real footage (C-44, permissioned) or, until it exists, the "To my grandchildren" letter page and a small hand-knit item. "There's a letter in this book addressed to grandchildren. Give it to the new grandparents. They'll write it before the baby can read."
- **CTA.** "The letter to my grandchildren."
- **Target.** Meta: new parents (life event: new parent, 0-12 months), expecting parents, baby-registry interests; women 26-40.
- **Landing.** Amazon.
- **Test variable.** Footage vs. page; "grandparents" vs. "new grandma."

## Test plan per concept

| Stage | Budget | Duration | Decide on |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Hook test | $10/day per platform, 3-5 variations | 4 days | Thumb-stop rate (3-second views ÷ impressions) and CTR. Kill under 25% thumb-stop or under 0.8% CTR. |
| 2. Body test | $20/day on the winning hook, 2-3 bodies | 4 days | Attribution clicks and Amazon "detail page views" per $. |
| 3. Scale | $30-100/day | Until cost per attributed purchase exceeds $6.50 for 3 days | Purchases from Amazon Attribution. |

Rule: never judge a concept on clicks alone. Amazon Attribution reports detail page views, add to
carts, and purchases; a concept that gets cheap clicks and no carts is entertainment, not an ad.

## Platform notes

- **Meta.** Start with Advantage+ audiences off and interest targeting on for the first month, so
  we learn which interests convert. Move to Advantage+ once 50 attributed purchases exist. Creative
  formats: 9:16 video for Reels and Stories, 4:5 for feed, carousel for AD-07.
- **TikTok.** Spark Ads on organic posts that already have comments beat dark posts in this category.
  Use "Smart Creative" with 3-5 videos per ad group. Keep the sign-off frame; TikTok ads without a
  brand cue get comments asking "what book?" and lose the click.
- **Pinterest.** Promoted pins on ST-05, ST-20, and ST-01 from November 1 and April 1. Pinterest
  buyers plan gifts early; the pin should say the occasion.
- **YouTube.** Not before there is a 25-question long video (C-20) to run as an in-feed ad.

## Amazon Ads (on the listing itself)

Already specified in `../../07-amazon/STAGE-10-amazon-listing-and-launch.md` section 12; repeated
here with the sequence that matches zero reviews:

1. **Now:** Sponsored Products, automatic targeting, $10/day, to harvest search terms. Review the
   search-term report weekly; move anything with a sale into the manual campaign.
2. **At 10 reviews:** Sponsored Products, manual exact match, top 30 buyer phrases: memory journal
   for mom, memory book for dad, grandparent memory book, gift for mom who has everything, legacy
   journal, life story book for grandparents, questions to ask your parents, family history journal,
   memory journal for grandma, gifts for 70th birthday mom. Bids $0.60 to $0.90.
3. **At 20 reviews:** Product targeting on the top 20 competing ASINs (the thumbnail contrast is
   the strategy; our ink cover next to their watercolor).
4. **Season:** double bids from November 15 to December 12 and April 20 to May 8.
5. **Never:** competitor brand names as keywords (KDP prohibits it). "Storyworth" and the like are
   off limits in copy and keywords.

Budget expectation: $300 in month one, $500 in month two, $1,000 to $2,000 in December, all
subject to the ACoS gate (35% during launch, 25% after).

## Creative production checklist for every ad

- Sign-off frame on the last 1.5 seconds.
- Caption text legible at phone size (the book's cream on ink passes).
- No claims: no "bestseller," no ratings, no "thousands of families."
- Attribution link unique to the ad and platform.
- Logged in `../CONTENT_DATABASE.md` with the ad ID, and in `../experiments/EXPERIMENTS.md` with the test variable.
