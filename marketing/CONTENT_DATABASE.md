# Content Database

Every idea the engine has produced, with its status and results. Before adding an idea, search this
file for its prompt and its hook. Rules: no prompt reused within 30 days; no hook reused within 30
days; nothing invented (no fake reactions, testimonials, or comments); the book appears as the
subject in at most 1 of 5 posts.

Fields per entry: ID · Concept · Hook · Format · Platform · Audience (segments from
`research/POSITIONING_ANALYSIS.md`) · Emotional trigger · Stop-scroll reason · Share reason ·
Comment reason · Book connection · CTA · Difficulty · Time · AI video? · Real footage better? ·
Series? · Paid potential · Status · Results · Notes.

Status values: idea · scripted · produced · posted · winning · failed · retired.

### C-01 · Not as a mother
- **Concept.** The book's first great line, alone, then its three sub-questions, one at a time.
- **Hook.** "Tell me about your mother, not as a mother."
- **Format.** Text video, 12 s. Cream type on ink. No voice, or a single quiet read.
- **Platform.** TikTok, Reels. **Audience.** A, B.
- **Emotional trigger.** Curiosity; the person before the role.
- **Stop-scroll.** The phrase contradicts itself and resolves in the reader's head.
- **Share.** "Send this to your mom with no comment."
- **Comment.** "What did she want?" is a question people answer for their own mothers.
- **Book connection.** Part One, page 1 prompt with sub-line "What was she like as a person? What did she want?"
- **CTA.** Ask her tonight.
- **Difficulty.** Low. **Time.** 30 min. **AI video?** Not needed. **Real footage better?** No. **Series?** S1 launch. **Paid.** High (text ads convert in this category).
- **Status.** idea. **Results.** none. **Notes.** Launch video. Test A: no sound. Test B: quiet piano.
